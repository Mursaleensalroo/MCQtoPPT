Format guide & example

Each question is a block starting with MCQ 1 (or Q1). Options use (A)…(D), the key is a Correct Answer: line, and the Why (X) is correct / incorrect lines become the explanation. An optional first line TOPIC: … sets the deck title, and Statement I/II/III… lines are grouped automatically. Load an example →

MCQ 1
Question:
Which book is maintained by the bank?
(A) Petty cash book
(B) Bank passbook
(C) Purchases day book
(D) Sales day book
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation:
Why (B) is correct:
The passbook is the bank's copy of the account.
Why (A) is incorrect:
Petty cash is recorded by the firm.
Why (C) is incorrect:
Purchases go in the purchases day book.
Why (D) is incorrect:
Sales go in the sales day book.
